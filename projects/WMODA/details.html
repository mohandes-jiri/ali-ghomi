WMODA

CetraRuddy · Lead Designer · 2023 · Schematic Design · South Florida

The Wiener Museum of Decorative Art (WMODA) development is a mixed-use project in South Florida, combining a residential building of 75 rental units and amenity spaces with a museum of glass and ceramic art. The museum design takes cues from the art itself: fluid, dynamic forms, shimmering reflection and refraction of light, and glaze. Sectional relationships inside and outside the museum are a key driver. A void over the parking draws light into the arrival space, and a green wall brings it to life while aiding air circulation. Internal voids between the three museum floors give visitors dynamic views of the art from different perspectives and give curators great flexibility.

The terracotta exterior is inspired by ceramic art, a material that allows both glazed effects and sculptural form. A dynamic three-dimensional pattern of faceted surfaces creates a rippling effect as the sun moves around the building, and is designed to be fabricated by extrusion rather than molding. The geometry was developed in Rhino and Grasshopper and brought into Revit using Rhino.Inside.Revit, enabling rapid testing, documentation and rationalization. A physical sample was also made to study shadows and highlights under different conditions.
