About

Ali Tabatabaie Ghomi is a designer working at the intersection of architecture, technology, and construction. His expertise lies in computational design, design rationalization, digital fabrication, and the automation of design workflows. He holds a Master of Science in Design from the University of Pennsylvania.

Ali is currently a Senior Computational Designer at HKS LINE Group, where he develops design strategies, rationalizes complex geometry, and builds interoperability and automation pipelines that facilitate coordination, communication, and project delivery. He has contributed to national and international sports and venue, hospitality, and healthcare projects, from concept through construction documents, including Browns Stadium, Washington Commanders Stadium, and FSCB Children Hospital.

Earlier in his career, he was a Senior Designer at CetraRudy Architecture, where he founded and led the computational design committee. The committee focused on research, education, and sustainable design, and its work supported projects including MottHaven Tower and Atir Towers. His projects have ranged from a 7,000 sq ft cultural center to a 3.1 million sq ft residential development.

Ali also teaches and conducts research. He is currently a professor at the University of Texas at Arlington (UTA). Previously, at the Polyhedral Structures Laboratory at the University of Pennsylvania, he was a research assistant and design lead, and led the design of Saltatur, which received the A' Design Award. He has also taught as a lecturer at the Stuart Weitzman School of Design and as a guest lecturer at the City College of New York. His Statera table has received Gold and Silver MUSE Design Awards.
