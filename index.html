About me

Ali Tabatabaie Ghomi is a designer and architect with expertise in computational and digital design and fabrication. He holds a Master's of Science in Design from the University of Pennsylvania. As wells as years of experience as a professional architectural designer, he is actively working in other fields such as furniture design, product design, experimental art, etc.
