Arse Simorgh
Arse Simorgh is a podcast about Persian literature. Simorgh is a mythical bird in Persian mythology with similar attributes to Phoenix. The logo is a combination of persian calligraphy combined with abstract brush strokes and splashes. The color pallet is inspired by the art of Tazhib.
